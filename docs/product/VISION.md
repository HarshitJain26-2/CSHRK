# CSHRK Product Vision

## 1. Executive Vision
CSHRK is a **cooperative-owned digital workforce and service marketplace**. It is designed specifically for **Labour Cooperative Federations and Labour Cooperative Societies** to organize workers, manage capacity, offer guaranteed services to institutions and citizens, and provide workers with fair wages, verifiable skills, and cooperative ownership benefits.

## 2. What CSHRK Is NOT
- **Not a generic gig-worker portal**: Workers are organized into legally registered cooperative societies, not atomized individual contractors.
- **Not an Urban Company or TaskRabbit clone**: The platform emphasizes collective bargaining, skill development, welfare protection, and transparent pricing rather than corporate commission extraction.
- **Not an ad-hoc AI chatbot**: AI is employed as a precision allocation and matching engine on top of real verified workforce data.

## 3. Product Layers Overview
1. **Marketplace Layer**: Customer booking, service requests, work fulfillment, verified invoicing, and dual-sided ratings.
2. **Cooperative Operations Layer**: Society management, worker membership records, Skill Passports, training certifications, welfare schemes, and settlement ledgers.
3. **AI Labour Intelligence Layer**: PostGIS geographic matching, demand forecasting, cooperative workforce capacity allocation, and regional skill gap analytics.
